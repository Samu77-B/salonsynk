## Four headline features (homepage)

1. **One diary** — Whole team’s appointments in one place; reduce double-booking and spreadsheet chaos.
2. **Your team, your clients** — Stylists, client records, colour formulas and notes in one system.
3. **Online booking** — Clients book 24/7 on a branded booking page; link from the salon website.
4. **No commissions** — Flat monthly fee; no per-booking cut from the platform.

## Pricing & plan highlights (as marketed)

- **£50/mo** per salon (flat fee; check site/config if you change pricing).
- Included messaging: **unlimited team members**, **unlimited clients**, **branded booking page**, **no commissions**.
- **Stripe** is optional: diary and bookings work without it; connect for **deposits** and **in-salon payments**.

## Product capabilities (detail — help / product)

Use these for longer copy, landing pages, or FAQs.

- **Diary** — Day view (per stylist) and week list; drag to reschedule/reassign; filters; add/edit appointments.
- **Team** — Roles, diary colours, profile photos; invite with email or display-only; activate/deactivate/delete.
- **Clients** — Database with notes and **colour formulas**; structured **colour history** (brand, formula, processing time, notes, image URL where used).
- **Checkout** — In-salon payments via **Stripe**; stylist, client, services; **silent appointment** option; cancellation policy where configured.
- **Settings** — Branding (logo, colours), booking page options, **public booking URL** (e.g. `salonsynk.com/book/your-salon`).
- **Communications** — Email reminders; **review requests** (optional Google review URL in marketing settings).
- **Booking experience** — Embeddable or linked booking; matches salon branding from settings (see integration docs).

**Advanced / roadmap-style items** (verify before claiming in ads): no-show/deposit settings, chair-renter split payments via Stripe Connect, smart gaps between appointments, “We Miss You” re-engagement, before/after photo URLs on appointments, retail **products** shop for salons, reports/PDF exports.
